Contains information from OpenFlights (https://openflights.org/data), available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
The accompanying reference-data.json is a transformed extract under ODbL 1.0. Names and supported codes are retained; missing codes are null. Airlines without a usable IATA/ICAO code and unknown/private-flight placeholders are omitted. Historical airlines are included. Active status is not inferred. No personal flight logs or photos are included. Manufacturer is not inferred.
